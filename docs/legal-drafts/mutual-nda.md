## 1. Purpose

The parties may share confidential information to carry out the review described in order form [number] (the Purpose).

## 2. Confidential information

Information that one party (the Discloser) gives the other (the Recipient) in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would treat as confidential. For the Customer it includes source code, schemas, query statistics, plans and the report. For the Author it includes working methods and scripts that are not public.

Not confidential: information that is public without the Recipient's fault, already known to the Recipient without a duty of confidence, received from a third party without a duty of confidence, or developed independently without use of the Discloser's information.

## 3. Duties

The Recipient will: use the information only for the Purpose; keep it as carefully as its own confidential information and at least reasonably; share it only with people who need it for the Purpose and are bound by equal duties; not copy it beyond what the Purpose needs; and not publish or name the Discloser or its systems.

## 4. Compelled disclosure

If the law or an authority requires disclosure, the Recipient tells the Discloser first where the law allows, and discloses only what is required.

## 5. Return and deletion

On request, and in any case within 30 days after delivery of the report, the Recipient deletes or returns the Discloser's information and confirms it in writing. A copy kept in a routine backup stays confidential until it is overwritten.

## 6. No personal data

The Customer will not provide production personal data. If personal data reaches the Author by mistake, the Author tells the Customer and deletes it. [Lawyer: data processing terms needed? See README question 3.]

## 7. Term

Duties last for [3] years from the date above. Duties about source code last until it becomes public through no fault of the Recipient.

## 8. Own work and other obligations

Nothing here stops either party from independent work, or obliges either party to enter an engagement. The Author performs the review outside any other engagement's hours and on equipment the Author owns, and uses no information from any other engagement.
